The founder

Every project on this site leads back to one person.

David Ranđelović is the founder of Redtekk and the engineer behind the work you see here. He spent the years before the studio building software for clients around the world: as an independent freelancer, through Xelpi-IT, a company he founded and ran, and as part of established agency teams.

Redtekk is the next chapter of that work: the same engineer, the same standards, now with a team around him. When you work with Redtekk, David is in the code and on the calls, not managing from a distance.
