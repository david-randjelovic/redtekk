Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RedTekk ("we", "us") handles information when you visit our website. We keep data collection to a minimum: this is a marketing website, we do not run user accounts, and we do not sell your data.

Who we are

RedTekk is a digital studio based in Serbia (RS). For any privacy question, contact us at hello@redtekk.com.

What we collect

  • Contact details: if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.
  • Booking details: if you schedule a meeting, scheduling is handled by Calendly, which collects the name, email, and time you provide to arrange the meeting.
  • Technical data: standard server and font-delivery requests (such as your IP address) that are necessary to load the page.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking pixels, and we do not build profiles about you.

Third parties we use

  • Calendly powers the "Schedule a meeting" scheduler and only loads after you allow functional cookies. See Calendly's own privacy policy for how they process booking data.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used across the site, which may involve a request to Google's servers that logs your IP address.

How we use information

We use the information solely to respond to your enquiries, schedule and hold calls you request, and keep the website functioning and secure. We do not use it for marketing automation or share it with advertisers.

Legal basis

We process contact and booking data on the basis of your request and our legitimate interest in responding to you. Functional cookies are set only with your consent.

Data retention

We keep email correspondence only for as long as needed to handle your request and our ongoing relationship, then delete it. Booking data is retained by Calendly per their retention policy.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@redtekk.com.

Cookies

For details on the cookies we use and how to control them, see our Cookie Policy. You can change your choice anytime via "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website evolves. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
