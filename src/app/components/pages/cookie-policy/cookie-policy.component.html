Cookie Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies we use on the Redtekk website and how you can control them. We use as few as possible.

What are cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device by your browser. They let a site remember choices and enable certain features.

Cookies we use

Essential

Always active. We store your cookie choice in a first-party cookie named redtekk_cookie_consent so we do not have to ask again on every visit. These are required for the site to work and cannot be turned off.

Functional

Optional, off by default. When you allow these, we load Calendly so you can book a call directly on the site. Calendly may set its own cookies to run the scheduler. If you decline, the scheduler is not loaded.

Third parties

  • Calendly: our booking tool, loaded only with functional consent.
  • Google Fonts: serves our typefaces. It does not set cookies, but loading fonts involves a request to Google that may log your IP address.

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Managing your choices

You can review or change your preferences at any time using the button below or the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. You can also clear or block cookies through your browser settings.

More information

For how we handle personal data more broadly, see our Privacy Policy, or contact us at hello@redtekk.com.
