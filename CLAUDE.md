# Code rules

## Content / copywriting

- **No em dashes (`—`) in user-facing text.** Use a comma instead. This applies to
  all copy rendered to users (component templates, service/section data strings,
  marketing copy). Code comments are exempt.
  - ✅ `scale with your business, not slow it down.`
  - ❌ `scale with your business — not slow it down.`
